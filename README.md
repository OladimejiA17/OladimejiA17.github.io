# Data Analytics Portfolio
# Project 1
**Title:** [England Regional Property Market Dashboard](England_Regional_Property_Market_Dashboard.xlsx)

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Table, Pivot Chart, Slicers)

**Project Description:** 
This project involved analysing England’s regional property market data to identify trends and patterns in house prices, and market performance across different regions. It is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of key property market metrics and enable stakeholders to easily monitor and compare market performance across regions and over time. The dashboard includes the following features:

Average House Price by Region: Visual representation of average property prices across different regions of England, highlighting variations in housing costs.

Property Price Trends Over Time: A time-based analysis of house prices, providing insights into how the property market has changed across different periods.

Additionally, the dashboard includes interactive filters and controls for:

Region: Focus on a specific region of England to analyse its property market performance.

Time Period: Filter the data by specific years or periods to examine changes and trends over time.

**Key findings:**
Regional Price Differences: Identified significant differences in property prices across England, with some regions consistently recording higher average house prices than others.

Regional Market Performance: Highlighted variations in property market activity across regions, helping to identify areas with stronger or weaker transaction levels.

Market Volatility: Analysed fluctuations in regional property prices and transaction activity, highlighting periods of stronger growth or slower market performance.

Regional Opportunities: The comparison of house prices and market activity helps identify regions that may offer potential opportunities for buyers, investors, and other property market stakeholders.

This dashboard serves as a valuable analytical tool for property investors, buyers, estate agents, and other stakeholders, providing clear and accessible insights into England’s regional property market. It supports informed decision-making by allowing users to compare regions, monitor market trends, and identify changes in property market performance over time.

# Project 2
**Title:** [BNPL Customer Insights Dashboard](Buy_Now_Pay_Later_Dashboard.pbix)

**Tools Used:** Power BI (Power query, DAX, Dashboard)

**Project Description:**
This project involved analysing Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) customer and credit-risk data to identify trends and patterns in customer behaviour, purchase activity, and repayment performance. It is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of key customer and financial metrics. This dashboard allows stakeholders to easily monitor and analyse BNPL customer activity across different customer segments, employment types, locations, and time periods. The dashboard includes the following features:

Purchase Amount by Product Category: Visual representation of total purchase amounts across different product categories, helping identify the categories generating the highest BNPL spending.

Customer Distribution by Segment: Displays the number of customers across different customer segments, providing insights into the composition of the BNPL customer base.

Customers by Employment Type: Highlights customer distribution by employment status, helping assess the relationship between employment characteristics and BNPL usage.

Purchase Amount by Location: Shows total purchase amounts across different locations, allowing stakeholders to identify geographical areas with higher BNPL activity.

Total Purchase Amount: Provides an overall view of the total value of purchases made through the BNPL service.

Average Credit Score: Displays the average customer credit score, providing an indication of the overall credit profile of the customer base.

Total Customers: Shows the total number of customers included in the analysis.

Additionally, the dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

Transaction Date: Filter the data by year, quarter, or month to analyse changes in BNPL activity over time.

Location: Focus on specific locations to compare customer activity and purchase behaviour geographically.

Repayment Status: Filter customers according to their repayment status to analyse repayment behaviour and credit-risk patterns.

**Key findings:**
Product Category Spending: Identified differences in purchase amounts across product categories, highlighting the categories that contribute most significantly to overall BNPL spending.

Customer Segmentation: Revealed the distribution of customers across different segments, providing insight into the types of customers most actively using BNPL services.

Employment Patterns: Highlighted variations in BNPL customer numbers across employment types, helping stakeholders understand the employment profile of BNPL users.

Geographical Differences: Identified locations with higher levels of BNPL purchase activity, supporting regional analysis and targeted business strategies.

Credit Profile: The average credit score provides an overview of the credit quality of the customer base and can be used alongside repayment status to assess potential credit-risk exposure.

Repayment Behaviour: Analysis of repayment status enables stakeholders to identify differences in repayment outcomes and monitor areas that may require closer credit-risk management.

Customer and Transaction Trends: The combination of total customers, purchase amounts, transaction dates, and interactive filters provides a clearer view of how BNPL usage changes across different periods and customer groups.

This dashboard serves as a valuable analytical tool for BNPL providers and financial stakeholders, providing clear, actionable insights into customer behaviour, purchasing activity, credit profiles, and repayment patterns. It supports informed decision-making in areas such as customer segmentation, credit-risk management, regional targeting, and BNPL service strategy.
